# Content attribution

Topic study pages contain generated adaptations or original factual summaries of their cited sources. Each page retains source titles, publishers, URLs, Wikipedia revision identifiers where available, retrieval metadata, and any verified license links. Multiple-source pages retain every listed reference. A source without a license label is a factual reference, not a claim that its text is openly licensed. Follow those page-level links for source attribution and applicable terms; a blanket license is not asserted for the combined corpus.

Original tournament questions and answers are retained with their tournament, round, question number, and school level. Bonus lead-ins and all three parts remain together. These texts are attributed to their original tournament sources; their inclusion here does not assert ownership or grant a new redistribution license.

Practice questions were generated and editorially revised from the topic descriptions, study content, and tournament questions. The original multiple-choice set retains its evidence references and canonical answer names; some incorrect choices intentionally use invented titles or names. The dedicated typed/recall set was generated with GPT-6 Luna using topic descriptions, study prose, and tournament context, then reviewed for automatically detected answer giveaways. It stores question stems and answers without model-generated citations or aliases. Saved question pages retain the supplied topic, study notes, and tournament sources as context; these are not individual claim-by-claim citations.

The dataset was prepared using OpenAI models. Later study-page enrichment used GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna with internet research, source checks, and automatic validation. The Luna enrichment used one independent request per topic; a 12-topic pilot sample received an independent factual audit, while the full 939-topic Luna set did not. Targeted, evidence-backed corrections were applied before import. Each accepted study block retains its supporting source URLs and page-level reference metadata without asserting an unverified source license. Detailed generation and review records are maintained separately from the published website.
