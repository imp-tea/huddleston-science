How reviews work

Successful reviews on separate days advance through 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days. Mistakes reset the interval to 1 day. Early successes and same-day retries do not advance it. A first success followed by two successful due reviews on separate days qualifies as remembered across reviews. Changed questions start fresh. Dates use America/Chicago.
